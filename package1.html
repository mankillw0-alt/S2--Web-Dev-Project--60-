Overview

Japan's ancient capital awaits

Kyoto is the soul of Japan — a city where centuries-old temples sit beside lantern-lit streets and seasonal cherry blossoms paint the sky pink. Our 6-night package takes you deep into the culture, cuisine and calm of this extraordinary destination. From Arashiyama's bamboo groves to Fushimi Inari's thousands of torii gates, every day feels like stepping into a living postcard.

6-Night Itinerary

Day by day

Day 1

Arrival & Gion District

Arrive at Kansai International Airport and transfer to your traditional ryokan in central Kyoto. Spend the evening exploring the Gion district, famous for its preserved machiya townhouses and geisha culture.

Day 2

Fushimi Inari & Nishiki Market

Early morning hike through the thousands of vermillion torii gates at Fushimi Inari Shrine. Afternoon stroll through Nishiki Market — Kyoto's bustling food street packed with local delicacies.

Day 3

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

Wander through the iconic bamboo forest at Arashiyama, visit the serene Tenryu-ji temple gardens, and take a traditional boat ride along the Hozu River.

Day 4

Kinkaku-ji & Tea Ceremony

Visit the breathtaking Golden Pavilion, Kinkaku-ji, shimmering over its reflection pond. Afternoon traditional Japanese tea ceremony hosted by a local tea master.

Day 5

Day Trip to Nara

Take a short train to Nara and walk among hundreds of freely roaming deer in Nara Park. Visit the spectacular Todai-ji temple housing Japan's largest bronze Buddha.

Day 6

Farewell Dinner & Departure

Final morning exploring any missed spots or shopping for souvenirs along Sannen-zaka. Farewell kaiseki dinner before your transfer to the airport.
