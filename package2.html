Overview

Paradise found in the Indian Ocean

The Maldives is the ultimate tropical escape — a scattering of 1,200 coral islands across the Indian Ocean, each more breathtaking than the last. Our 5-night package sets you up in a private overwater villa above a crystal-clear lagoon teeming with marine life. Whether you want to dive, snorkel, kayak or simply do absolutely nothing, the Maldives delivers pure, unfiltered paradise.

5-Night Itinerary

Day by day

Day 1

Arrival & Seaplane Transfer

Fly into Velana International Airport and board a scenic seaplane transfer to your private island resort. Check into your overwater villa and enjoy a welcome dinner as the sun sets over the lagoon.

Day 2

Snorkelling & Reef Exploration

Guided morning snorkel directly from your villa steps into the house reef. Swim alongside sea turtles, reef sharks and colourful tropical fish. Afternoon at leisure by your private pool.

Day 3

Dolphin Cruise & Sandbank Picnic

Morning cruise to spot spinner dolphins in the open ocean. Afternoon picnic on a deserted sandbank — just white sand, turquoise water and you.

Day 4

Spa Day & Water Sports

Full-body treatment at the overwater spa. Afternoon choice of kayaking, paddleboarding, windsurfing or jet skiing across the lagoon.

Day 5

Farewell Dinner & Departure

Final morning soaking up the Maldivian sun. Private beach farewell dinner at sunset, followed by your seaplane transfer back to the main island for departure.
