Overview

Overwater bliss in the South Pacific

Set in the heart of French Polynesia, Bora Bora is one of the world's most sought-after island destinations. Famous for its turquoise lagoon, dramatic Mount Otemanu, and luxurious overwater bungalows — this is the escape you've always imagined. Our package gives you 7 unforgettable nights with everything arranged, so all you do is arrive and unwind.

7-Night Itinerary

Day by day

Day 1

Arrival & Check-In

Arrive at Bora Bora Airport, transfer to your overwater bungalow. Relax and watch the sunset over the lagoon with a welcome cocktail.

Day 2

Lagoon Snorkelling Tour

Join a guided boat tour of the Bora Bora lagoon. Snorkel with manta rays and sharks. Lunch on a private motu sandbar island.

Day 3

Mount Otemanu Hike

Trek the trails around the iconic volcanic peak with a local guide. Panoramic views of the island and ocean at the top.

Days 4-6

Free Exploration Days

Spa treatments, jet ski rentals, paddleboarding, parasailing, or simply lounging in your private plunge pool.

Day 7

Farewell Dinner & Departure

Private sunset dinner on the beach. Transfer to the airport for your homeward flight, with memories to last a lifetime.
